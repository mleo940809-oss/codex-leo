# Stage and Template Selection

## Use the lightweight template when

- The work is still exploring a need, problem, scope, or candidate solution.
- The document is meant to capture early ideas, not a final handoff.
- The task may still change materially after discussion.

## Use the standard template when

- The work is entering formal review, freeze, delivery, or archive.
- The requirements must be precise, testable, and ready for execution.
- The document is expected to become a formal product contract.

## Selection rules

- Do not mix exploration and formal delivery in the same file.
- When a document crosses into formal review or freeze, switch to the standard template.
- If the task explicitly asks for a full PRD or final delivery, default to the standard template.
